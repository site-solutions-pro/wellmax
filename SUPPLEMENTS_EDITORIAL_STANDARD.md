# WellMax Education — Scientific Supplement Standard

Status: approved editorial architecture (2026-08-08)

Every long-form supplement dossier must be evidence-led and separate established human outcomes from mechanistic, preclinical, surrogate and speculative claims.

## Required 22-section architecture
1. Executive summary
2. What the molecule/nutrient does in the body
3. Molecular mechanism
4. Organs and physiological systems
5. Benefits supported by stronger human evidence
6. Possible benefits / limited or mixed evidence
7. Popular claims without adequate evidence
8. Deficiency / low-status states and risk groups (when applicable)
9. Laboratory tests and biomarkers
10. Dietary requirements, AI/RDA/UL and studied exposures (when applicable)
11. Protocol framework by objective — educational, evidence-based, not individualized prescribing
12. Timing, food and combinations
13. Chemical forms, formulation and bioavailability
14. How to choose a quality supplement
15. Drug interactions
16. Adverse effects
17. High-dose use and toxicity
18. Special populations and clinical contexts
19. Dose/exposure × objective × evidence table
20. FAQ
21. Scientific references, prioritizing primary trials, systematic reviews and official sources
22. Contextual CTAs to related WellMax dossiers

## Language parity
Every dossier is planned for full scientific parity in Portuguese (PT-BR), English (EN) and Spanish (ES). Translation must preserve evidence boundaries, safety caveats, tables and references rather than becoming a shortened summary.

## Evidence rules
- Human clinical outcomes > validated surrogate outcomes > mechanistic human data > animal/in-vitro evidence.
- Association is not causation; biomarker change is not automatically a clinical benefit.
- Clearly distinguish nutritional replacement, supplementation and pharmacologic/high-dose exposure.
- Do not imply that experimental longevity mechanisms are proven to extend human lifespan.
- Regulatory status must be explicit for investigational molecules.
- Avoid individualized diagnosis, prescribing, sourcing or self-medication instructions.

## Quality / product selection
When relevant evaluate elemental amount or active amount, dose per serving, chemical form, stability/oxidation, contaminant testing, independent certification, batch traceability, storage and misleading front-label quantities.

## Cross-link clusters
- Healthy aging / cellular energetics: NMN → NAD+ → NADH → TMG → resveratrol → PQQ → CoQ10.
- Performance: EAAs → beta-alanine → L-carnitine → magnesium.
- Neuro / relaxation: L-theanine → GABA → magnesium.
- Micronutrients: zinc ↔ copper context; iron ↔ vitamin C; vitamin D ↔ magnesium; B6/B9/B12 ↔ one-carbon metabolism.
