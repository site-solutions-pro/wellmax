Interpretation framework

Four questions before drawing conclusions

01

What is measured?

Understand the assay, unit and biological signal.

02

What changes it?

Consider timing, illness, medication, physiology and measurement variability.

03

What is the evidence?

Separate association, prediction, mechanism and intervention evidence.

04

What is the context?

Interpret results with qualified clinical guidance and the wider health picture.
