Brain health

Sleep, cognition and resilience

The brain depends on blood flow, sleep, metabolic health and use. Several dementia risk factors are modifiable, while most brain-boosting supplements and peptides remain unproven.

Key systems

Sleep

Sleep supports memory consolidation and metabolic clearance in the brain.

Vascular health

Blood pressure, glucose and lipids affect small vessels that feed brain tissue.

Stress physiology

The HPA axis and cortisol shape mood, sleep and appetite.

Nerve health

Peripheral nerves need B12, glucose control and blood flow to work normally.

Evidence

What has the strongest human evidence

  • The 2024 Lancet Commission lists 14 modifiable risk factors, including hearing loss, high LDL cholesterol, physical inactivity and hypertension.
  • Aerobic exercise increased hippocampal volume in a randomized trial in older adults.
  • Correcting a documented B12 deficiency can prevent or improve neurological symptoms.
  • Regular sleep of seven hours or more is recommended for adults.

How to read the evidence

Cognitive claims are hard to test: they need validated cognitive outcomes and long follow-up. Short studies on mood or alertness do not show protection against dementia.
