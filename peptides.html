GH-axis profiles

GHRH and ghrelin-receptor research

Approved

Tesamorelin

GHRH analog with an FDA-licensed indication for adults with HIV-associated excess abdominal fat.

Early human

CJC-1295

Long-acting GHRH analog with controlled endocrine pharmacology studies but no established clinical-outcome indication.

Phase II

Ipamorelin

Ghrelin-receptor agonist with a randomized postoperative ileus trial that did not establish efficacy.

Scientific reading

Do not collapse different evidence types into one claim

H

Human evidence

Population, comparator, endpoint and duration matter.

A

Animal evidence

Useful for hypotheses, not proof of human outcomes.

C

Cell evidence

Mechanistic signals under controlled laboratory conditions.

R

Regulatory status

Approval, registration and commercial availability are different concepts.
