Health Hub

Start with the foundations

Sleep, movement, nutrition and stress management have more human evidence than any supplement or peptide. Use this hub to understand the basics, then go deeper into each area.

The four foundations

Sleep

Seven hours or more on a regular schedule is the adult consensus recommendation.

Movement

WHO recommends regular aerobic activity plus muscle strengthening on two or more days a week.

Nutrition

A varied diet built on minimally processed foods covers most nutrient needs.

Stress and recovery

Chronic stress affects sleep, appetite, blood pressure and mood.
