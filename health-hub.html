Designed safety boundary

Personalized does not mean unsupervised.

WellMax can organize information, surface patterns and prepare questions. It does not diagnose, prescribe, set doses or create self-administration instructions.

The production product will require privacy controls, consent records, audit logs, professional review and validation before sensitive health-data features are activated.
