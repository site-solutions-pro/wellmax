ARA-290 / Cibinetide

ARA-290, also called cibinetide, is a synthetic peptide derived from the tissue-protective region of erythropoietin. It was designed to activate an innate repair receptor complex without reproducing erythropoietin's red-blood-cell-stimulating activity.

Evidence snapshot

Early randomized human evidence

Small phase II studies in sarcoidosis-associated small-fiber neuropathy reported changes in symptoms and nerve-fiber measures, but the evidence base remains limited and indication-specific.

Overview

A non-erythropoietic tissue-protective peptide

Cibinetide was engineered to explore tissue-protective signaling associated with erythropoietin biology while minimizing hematopoietic effects.

01

Molecule class

Short synthetic peptide derived from the helix-B surface region of erythropoietin.

02

Research target

Innate repair receptor signaling associated with anti-inflammatory and tissue-repair responses in experimental models.

03

Human evidence

Small randomized phase II studies have focused on neuropathic symptoms and nerve-fiber measures in sarcoidosis.

04

Key boundary

Early symptom and surrogate biomarker changes do not establish broad neuroregeneration, disease modification or efficacy in unrelated conditions.

Mechanism

Innate repair receptor signaling

IRR

Repair signaling

ARA-290 was designed to engage a tissue-protective receptor complex associated with cytoprotective and anti-inflammatory signaling rather than classical erythropoietic signaling.

Nerve

Small-fiber biology

Preclinical and human studies have explored whether this signaling can support small nerve fibers and improve neuropathic symptoms.

Boundary

Mechanism ≠ efficacy

Biological plausibility and changes in nerve-fiber measurements require confirmation against durable patient-centered clinical outcomes.

Human evidence

Sarcoidosis-associated small-fiber neuropathy

Pilot RCT

22 participants

A small randomized double-blind placebo-controlled study in sarcoidosis with small-fiber neuropathy reported improvement in a neuropathy symptom score with ARA-290, while several other outcomes improved similarly in both groups.

Phase 2b

64 participants

A later randomized study evaluated corneal nerve-fiber area as the primary endpoint and also assessed pain and physical function.

Surrogate endpoint

Nerve-fiber measures

Corneal and skin nerve-fiber measurements are biologically relevant but do not by themselves prove durable disease modification or functional benefit.

Other research

Metabolic and ophthalmic studies

Clinical registries include exploratory programs in metabolic and diabetic eye disease settings. Evidence should remain indication-specific.

Safety and limitations

Small studies limit certainty

Early studies did not identify major safety concerns within their small samples and short follow-up, but those trials were not powered to characterize uncommon, delayed or population-specific harms. There is no approved prescribing label defining contraindications, interactions or long-term risk.

Regulatory status

Investigational

Verified Aug 8, 2026: ARA-290/cibinetide remains an investigational research compound in the sources reviewed. Clinical trial registration and phase II research do not constitute marketing authorization.
