Mechanism

Three signaling pathways

The scientific rationale combines incretin-related signaling with glucagon-receptor activity. The net clinical effect depends on dose, population, trial design and tolerability.

GIP

Glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide receptor

One component of the molecule's incretin-based activity.

GLP-1

Glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor

A pathway already used by approved metabolic medicines, while retatrutide itself remains a separate investigational molecule.

GCG

Glucagon receptor

Adds a distinct metabolic signal that is part of the molecule's research hypothesis.

Context

Integrated response

Receptor activity should not be interpreted as a guaranteed clinical benefit outside controlled trials.

Safety context

How to read adverse-event data

Safety findings must be taken directly from trial publications and official study records. Readers should distinguish common adverse events, serious adverse events, treatment discontinuations, dose-dependent effects and events judged related or unrelated by investigators.

This page intentionally avoids individualized risk assessment, prescribing advice, compounded-product guidance and self-administration instructions.
