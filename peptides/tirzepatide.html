Mechanism

Dual incretin signaling

Tirzepatide activates GIP and GLP-1 receptors. Clinical outcomes reflect integrated effects on glucose-dependent insulin secretion, appetite, energy intake and metabolic physiology.

GIP

GIP receptor agonism

Contributes to glucose-dependent insulinotropic signaling and the molecule's combined pharmacology.

Supports glucose-dependent insulin secretion and reduced energy intake through established incretin pathways.

Safety and limitations

How to interpret harms

In SURMOUNT-1, the most common adverse events were gastrointestinal and were usually mild to moderate, occurring mainly during dose escalation. Treatment discontinuation because of adverse events was reported in 4.3%, 7.1% and 6.2% of the 5 mg, 10 mg and 15 mg groups, compared with 2.6% with placebo.

Contraindications, boxed warnings, precautions, drug interactions and population-specific risks must be taken from the current official prescribing information. Trial averages do not replace individualized medical assessment.
