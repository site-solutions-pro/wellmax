# Preparation and Dose Content Policy

Status date: 2026-11-18

Authority: owner decision. This policy replaces the earlier blanket prohibition on preparation and dose content.

## Allowed on molecule pages

1. **Preparation of lyophilized vials.** Reconstitution principles, aseptic technique, diluent selection by formulation, concentration arithmetic (mg ÷ mL), labeling, storage and stability guidance when tied to the specific formulation, and visual inspection of the solution.
2. **Label dosing for approved medicines.** The dosing schedule published in the official label (FDA, EMA or Anvisa), with the regulator, product name, indication and verification date. Label dosing is reported as what the label states, not as advice to the reader.
3. **Study doses for investigational compounds.** Doses used in each arm of published clinical trials, with trial name, phase, population, duration, outcomes and citation.
4. **Explicit absence.** When no human dose has been established, the page says so plainly.

## Not allowed

- Dose recommendations for the reader, including doses invented for compounds without established human dosing.
- Treatment cycles, on/off schedules or protocols not taken from a label or a cited trial.
- Stacks or combinations of compounds.
- Sourcing, purchasing or vendor guidance, including links to sellers.
- Copied text from third-party sites.

## Required warning

Every page with preparation or dose content shows the standard warning block (`.wm-warning`) near the top of the page, below the hero. Text by locale is defined below.

### Warning text

- **PT-BR:** Conteúdo educacional. As informações de preparo e as doses descritas vêm de bulas oficiais ou de estudos clínicos e não são recomendação de uso. Peptídeos sem registro na Anvisa não são aprovados para uso humano no Brasil. Não use nenhuma substância injetável sem acompanhamento de um médico.
- **EN:** Educational content. Preparation information and the doses described come from official labels or clinical studies and are not a recommendation for use. Investigational peptides are not approved for human use. Do not use any injectable substance without supervision from a physician.
- **ES:** Contenido educativo. La información de preparación y las dosis descritas provienen de prospectos oficiales o de estudios clínicos y no son una recomendación de uso. Los péptidos en investigación no están aprobados para uso humano. No utilice ninguna sustancia inyectable sin la supervisión de un médico.

## Review

Dose tables require a citation per row and a verification date. Existing pages flagged in `docs/SAFETY_CONTENT_REVIEW.md` are reviewed against this policy rather than removed.
