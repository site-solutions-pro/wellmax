B-complex vitamins

B-complex products combine the eight B vitamins: thiamin (B1), riboflavin (B2), niacin (B3), pantothenic acid (B5), B6, biotin (B7), folate (B9) and B12. Each has its own profile on WellMax.

Class
Group of eight water-soluble vitamins
Evidence summary
Correcting specific deficiencies: established. Combined B-vitamin products for heart disease or energy in replete adults: not shown.
Review status
Agent draft, 2026-10-31. Scientific review required.

What it does in the body

B vitamins work as coenzymes in energy metabolism, DNA synthesis, red blood cell formation and nerve function. Folate, B12 and B6 together regulate homocysteine.

Who may be at risk of deficiency

  • Older adults (especially B12).
  • Vegans (B12) and people planning pregnancy (folate).
  • Alcohol use disorder (thiamin, folate, B6).
  • After bariatric surgery or with malabsorption.
  • Long-term metformin or acid-reducing medicines (B12).

Forms in supplements

Products vary widely in which vitamins they contain and in what amounts, often far above the RDA for some vitamins.

What human trials show

  • RandomizedHOPE-2 and other trials: folic acid, B6 and B12 lowered homocysteine but did not reduce major cardiovascular events.
  • RandomizedVITACOG (271 older adults with mild cognitive impairment): B vitamins slowed brain atrophy, mainly in people with high homocysteine; cognitive outcomes were secondary.
  • Evidence unavailableThere is no reliable evidence that B-complex products increase energy in people without deficiency.

Observational evidence

  • High homocysteine is associated with cardiovascular disease and dementia, but lowering it with B vitamins has not reduced heart events.

Safety, upper limits and interactions

  • Check total B6 intake: some products contain amounts above the EFSA upper level.
  • High folic acid can mask B12 deficiency.
  • Niacin as nicotinic acid can cause flushing.

Regulatory context

  • United States: dietary supplements do not need FDA approval for safety or effectiveness before sale; manufacturers are responsible for safety and label claims.
  • Brazil: food supplements are regulated by Anvisa (RDC 243/2018 and its normative instructions), which define permitted ingredients, minimum and maximum amounts and allowed claims.

Limits of the evidence

Combination products make it hard to know which vitamin, if any, caused an effect. A deficiency in one B vitamin is better identified and addressed specifically.

This profile is educational. It does not recommend doses, products or brands. Talk to a health professional before starting supplements, especially if you take medicines, are pregnant or have a chronic condition.
