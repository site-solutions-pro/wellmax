Future roadmap

The subscription loop

Useful once.
More useful over time.

A WellMax membership is being shaped as an ongoing learning and review process, not a static PDF or a black-box answer.

  1. 01
    Set the objective

    Define outcomes, constraints and the decisions you are actually trying to support.

  2. 02
    Connect permissioned data

    Add compatible laboratory records and wearable history only with clear consent.

  3. 03
    Receive a traceable brief

    See trends, evidence strength, uncertainties and questions worth reviewing.

  4. 04
    Review and refine

    Clinical decisions stay with qualified professionals; the context evolves as new data arrives.

What a subscription will not buy

No hidden certainty.
No autonomous prescribing.

Payment will not turn incomplete data into a diagnosis or experimental research into a recommendation. Medication, supplement and peptide decisions require appropriate clinical judgment.

Launch depends on validated privacy, security, consent, billing and human-review controls. Until then, every paid capability shown here is explicitly a product plan.
